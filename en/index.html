The insulin dose formula

Meal dose = carbs (g) ÷ ICR  (or CU × U per CU)
Correction = (Glucosecurrent − Glucosetarget) ÷ ISF
Bolus = Meal dose + Correction → rounded to pen step

Example: 45 g of carbs, ICR 1 U : 10 g, glucose 170 mg/dL, target 120, ISF 50 mg/dL per unit. Meal: 45 ÷ 10 = 4.5 U; correction: (170 − 120) ÷ 50 = 1.0 U; total 5.5 U.

FAQ

How do I calculate my mealtime insulin dose?

Meal dose = grams of carbs ÷ insulin-to-carb ratio (or carb units × units per carb unit). Add a correction = (current glucose − target) ÷ insulin sensitivity factor when you are above target. InsulinCalc does this step by step and rounds to your pen step.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your own Google Sheet in your Google Drive. The site has no database of health data and can only access files it created or that you picked. See our privacy policy.

Is it suitable for children with type 1 diabetes?

Yes. It was built by parents of a child with T1D. Several caregivers can share one log by sharing the Google Sheet. All ratios must be prescribed by your doctor.

Does it support mg/dL?

Yes. Choose mmol/L or mg/dL and enter carbs in grams or carb units (10, 12 or 15 g) in Settings.

Can I try it without signing in?

Yes — demo mode stores entries in your browser only. Sign in with Google for a permanent log.
